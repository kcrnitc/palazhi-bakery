# Palazhi Bakery — Official Website

Artisanal bakery and authentic Kerala delicacies website for **Palazhi Bakery**, located in Thirunelloor, Cherthala, Alappuzha, Kerala.

## ✨ Features
- **Artisan Warm Gourmet Theme**: Rich espresso, honey amber, warm sourdough cream, and spiced terracotta aesthetics.
- **Dynamic Store Hours**: Live calculation of bakery opening status (7:00 AM – 9:00 PM IST).
- **Interactive 3D Tilt**: Physics-based perspective tilt on the hero bakery showcase.
- **Menu Filter**: Filter between Hot Snacks, Bakes & Biscuits, Tea & Breakfast, and Gifts & Sweets.
- **Cinematic Kerala Gallery**: Touch & drag carousel showcasing traditional Kerala delicacies (Puttu & Kadala, Ghee Roast Dosa, Kerala Sadhya, and Malabar Chai).
- **Direct WhatsApp Ordering**: Interactive order popup generating pre-formatted WhatsApp orders directly to `+91 85473 17272`.
- **Responsive**: Fully optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.
